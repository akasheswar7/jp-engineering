Corporate Leadership

From The Founder & Director's Desk

JP Engineering Enterprises started in February 2022, with an objective to be a leading player in Sales & Services of Air-Conditioning Systems for the North Coastal region of Andhra Pradesh.

We have established and grown ourselves in a very short period. Proud to say we have received Dealerships from Daikin, LG, OGeneral & Hitachi within a short span of 3 months, considering our past history, excellent customer experience, and the best quality of service delivery.

We at JP Engineering Enterprises provide turnkey solutions for all brand Air-Conditioners. We do Design, Supply, Installation, Ducting, Testing and Commissioning for Residential & Commercial Air conditioners. We repair and service units following international standards and refrigeration protocols with brand-trained professional technicians.

Our Customer's feedback is our progress card, and we always make sure we maintain 100% honesty and transparency. We do not charge customers with hidden charges or false diagnosis—we make customers aware of issues through videos and pictures.

Our multi-brand showroom in Seethammadhara is the only showroom and customer experience center in Vizag with live units of all brands installed, where customers can truly experience and make their own choice. Our service center is equipped with all necessary tools used for repairs, bearing top brands like Bosch, Dewalt & Taparia.

Our Mission is to be a leading Air conditioning Sales and service provider with the best quality service to make sure every system works trouble-free. Our Vision is to capture the North Coastal Air-Conditioning Market with 100% customer satisfaction.

Santosh Jayanthi
