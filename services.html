Our Services

Comprehensive HVAC solutions from expert consultation and equipment supply to professional installation, ducting engineering, and dependable maintenance.

AC Sourcing & Dealer Supply

Authorized supply of Daikin, LG, and Hitachi 5-star inverter split ACs, cassette units, and VRV/VRF systems at competitive prices.

Installation & Commissioning

Precision copper piping, outdoor unit mounting, nitrogen pressure testing, vacuuming, and airflow balancing by trained technicians.

VRV / VRF System Design

Customized multi-zone VRV/VRF ductable solutions with individual thermostat controllers for corporate offices, hotels, and hospitals.

Ducting & Ventilation

GI and PI duct engineering, acoustic thermal insulation, diffuser grill placement, and fresh air exhaust systems.

Maintenance & Servicing (AMC)

Comprehensive and non-comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts, filter wash, coil chemical cleaning, and refrigerant top-up.

Heat Load Estimation & Audits

Detailed site survey and room heat load calculation to recommend exact tonnage required for maximum energy efficiency.
